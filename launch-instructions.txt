TMG Home Solutions Inc. — Unofficial website concept

LOCAL PREVIEW
Double-click preview.command on macOS (requires Python 3).
Then open http://127.0.0.1:4207 in your browser.
Keep the Terminal window open; Control-C stops the server.

Alternatively, from this folder run:
python3 -m http.server 4207 --bind 127.0.0.1 --directory public

There is no build step or package installation. All assets are local.
The phone and email links open your device's phone or email application.
The project inquiry section gives guidance for composing an email; it does
not collect or submit data. No analytics or third-party fonts are used.
